Common questions

Questions people usually ask.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mainly e-commerce sellers (Amazon, eBay, Shopify), retail stores and restaurants, but really any business owner who needs a digital or AI solution. If your business runs on slow, manual processes, it can be automated: AI automations, business analytics dashboards, custom management apps and web apps. I work remotely with clients in the USA, UK, Europe and Pakistan.

How much does a custom dashboard or app cost?

It depends on scope. An AI automation or a business analytics dashboard (like an Amazon sales dashboard or Power BI report) costs less than a full retail store management system or restaurant management app with user roles. Tell me what you need on WhatsApp and you'll get a clear, fixed-price quote before any work starts. The first consultation is always free.

How long does a project take?

A focused analytics dashboard (an Amazon sales dashboard, e-commerce report or Power BI dashboard) typically takes days, not months. A complete management system for a retail store or restaurant, with user roles and reports, takes a few weeks. I build fast, test against your real business data, and show you working versions along the way.

Do you work remotely with clients in the USA and Europe?

Yes, I provide remote AI automation services and custom app development for businesses in the USA, UK, Europe and Pakistan. Being based in Karachi means my working day overlaps European business hours completely and US East Coast mornings, so communication stays same-day. Everything is delivered online: hosted web apps, shared dashboards and updates over WhatsApp or email.

Do you build real estate agency software?

Yes, I build multi-tenant real estate platforms that run a whole agency from one dashboard: property listings, client and lead pipelines, viewings, offers, deals and agent commission tracking, replacing spreadsheets and scattered WhatsApp threads. See REOS in the projects section for an example.

Can you build an interactive product or marketing website?

Yes, beyond dashboards and management apps, I build interactive, animated product and marketing sites: cost calculators, configurators, exploded/interactive product views and conversion-focused landing pages, like the Eco Bike project in my portfolio.
