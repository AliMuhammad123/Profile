Common questions

Answers before you ask.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mainly e-commerce sellers (Amazon, eBay, Shopify), retail stores and restaurants — but if your business has a slow, manual process, it can probably be automated. I work remotely with clients in the USA, UK, Europe and Pakistan.

How much does a custom dashboard or app cost?

It depends on scope — a focused analytics dashboard is much lighter than a full management system with user accounts. Tell me what you need on WhatsApp and I'll give you a clear, fixed quote before any work starts. The first conversation is always free.

How long does a project take?

A focused dashboard typically takes days, not months. A complete management app with roles and reports takes a few weeks. I build fast, test against your real data, and you see working versions along the way — no long silences.

Do you work remotely with clients in the USA and Europe?

Yes — fully remote. Being based in Karachi means my working day overlaps European business hours completely and US East Coast mornings/afternoons, so communication stays same-day. Everything is delivered online: hosted apps, shared dashboards, and updates over WhatsApp or email.
